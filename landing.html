How can I raise my CGPA from 6.8 to 7.5 in 2 semesters?
Analysis: You currently have a CGPA of 6.8. To reach 7.5 in 2 semesters, I need to know your remaining credits.

Assuming 40 credits remain (20 per semester):
Required average SGPA = (7.5 x total_credits - 6.8 x completed_credits) / 40

Result: You need approximately 8.85 SGPA in each of the next 2 semesters.
This is achievable with strong performance — aim for A+ in high-credit subjects.

The deterministic calculation engine (SGPA, CGPA, target predictor, backlog simulator) works completely offline once loaded. The AI chat's open-ended mode uses OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, which requires an internet connection and your API key — but the math-based fallback always runs locally.
